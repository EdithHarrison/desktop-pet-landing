Desktop Pet is back

Hi everyone!

It’s been almost a year since the last Desktop Pet update. Here’s what has been happening behind the scenes.

A Little Life Update

Earlier this year I landed a new job in the tech industry! It has been exciting, but learning a new role kept me busy, so I had less time for Desktop Pet than I wanted.

I never forgot about this little project. I was surprised to see people still subscribing and waiting for updates, and that motivated me to pick up where I left off.

What’s New in Desktop Pet?

I’ve been working on making Desktop Pet easier to use, more customizable, and even smarter!

Here’s what’s included in the latest update:

  • Simplified Windows Installation

    No more extracting ZIP files! Desktop Pet now offers a more straightforward installation process for Windows users, making it easier to get started.

  • Uninstall Directly from the App

    You can now uninstall Desktop Pet directly from within the application. The uninstall process removes the app from your computer, including its files in the original download location.

    Important: Uninstalling Desktop Pet also permanently deletes your account information, saved settings, and previously granted permissions. If you reinstall the app later, you’ll need to set everything up again.

  • Day & Night Themes

    Menus and dialogs now support light and dark appearances for a more comfortable experience.

  • Improved AI Assistant

    I’ve made improvements to the AI assistant to enhance your experience interacting with your desktop companion.

  • More AI Providers

    Alongside the existing OpenAI integration, Desktop Pet now also supports Claude and Grok, giving you more flexibility to choose your preferred AI provider.

What’s Next?

And this time, I don’t plan on disappearing for another year!

I’m committed to ongoing development and more regular updates. I can’t promise a fixed release schedule yet, but I’ll keep you posted on new features and what’s coming next.
