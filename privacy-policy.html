Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. What Desktop Pet collects

Desktop Pet does not have an account server, and the app does not send your files, chats, or settings to us.

The app saves these on your computer, in your home folder:

  • Optional local account: a username, a password hash, and an email only if you type one. You can also skip login.
  • Settings: pet, theme, volume, notification font size, timers, reminders, and todos.
  • API keys: the OpenAI, Claude, or Grok key you save. The key stays in a local config file.

This website collects information only when you choose to give it:

  • Newsletter: your email, if you subscribe. MailerLite stores that address and sends the updates.
  • Feedback: whatever you type into the feedback form. That form is hosted by Google.
  • Donations: Patreon and Ko-fi handle payment details. We do not receive or store card numbers.

2. What leaves your device

Some features only work by contacting another company’s service. Those requests go from your computer to that service. They do not come to us.

  • Chat: the message you send, a short recent history, and your API key go to the provider you picked: OpenAI, Anthropic for Claude, or xAI for Grok. Their own privacy policy covers that request.
  • Voice: when you use voice chat or the wake word, the recording is sent to Google speech recognition so it can be turned into text. The app does not keep the recording.
  • Weather: a city you name is sent to the weather service. If you do not name a city, the app asks an IP location service for an approximate city, then requests the forecast. That approximate city can be the city of your internet provider, not your street address.

The microphone is used only while voice features are on. Weather and chat need an internet connection. The pet can still sit on your desktop without those features.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • The app has no advertising and no analytics tracker.
  • Chats are kept in memory for the current session so the pet can follow the conversation. They are not written into a permanent chat history.
  • We cannot see your API key, your chats, your voice recordings, or the weather location lookup.

4. Third-party services

  • OpenAI, Anthropic, and xAI: chat, using the key you provide.
  • Google: speech recognition for voice features.
  • OpenWeatherMap, wttr.in, and Open-Meteo: weather. OpenWeatherMap is called with the app’s own weather key, not your chat key.
  • ipapi.co, ipinfo.io, and ip-api.com: an approximate city when a weather question does not name one.
  • GitHub: hosts the download files. Downloading the app does not require an account.
  • MailerLite, Google Forms, Patreon, and Ko-fi: newsletter, feedback, and optional donations.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Skip login and use the pet without a local account
  • Delete the local account, settings, and API keys by choosing Uninstall in the pet menu, or by deleting the app’s files in your home folder
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time
  • Cancel any Patreon subscription or stop Ko-fi donations at any time through those platforms
  • All tips and donations are completely voluntary and optional
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

6. Children's Privacy

Desktop Pet is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
