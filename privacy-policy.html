Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Desktop Pet is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • OpenAI API Key: Stored locally on your device, never transmitted to our servers
  • Feedback and Bug Reports: Only when you voluntarily submit them through our Google form
  • Newsletter Subscription: Email address only when you voluntarily subscribe to receive updates about new versions
  • Settings: Your preferences stored locally on your device

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the Desktop Pet service (all processing happens on your device)
  • To respond to support requests and improve the app based on your feedback
  • To fix bugs and issues you report through our feedback form
  • To send you email updates about new versions (only if you subscribe to our newsletter)
  • We do not collect or process your data for any other purposes

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • We do not have access to your OpenAI API key
  • We do not operate any servers - everything runs on your computer
  • No data is transmitted to us or our servers
  • Your data never leaves your device

4. Third-Party Services

Desktop Pet integrates with:

  • OpenAI API: For AI-powered features. Your API key is sent directly from your device to OpenAI's servers. We do not see or store your API key.
  • GitHub Actions: For automated builds and distribution (only for downloading the app, not for data collection)
  • Patreon and Ko-fi: For donation processing. All payment information and donor data is handled by these platforms. We do not collect or store any payment information.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (stored locally on your device)
  • Delete your data (uninstall the app)
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time
  • Cancel any Patreon subscription or stop Ko-fi donations at any time through those platforms
  • All tips and donations are completely voluntary and optional
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

6. Children's Privacy

Desktop Pet is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at:

  • Email: support@desktoppet.app
